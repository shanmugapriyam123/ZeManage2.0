How we calculated this

Numbers are built from per-user-per-month rates observed across reference Revit teams (e.g., ~6 critical-element blocks, ~120 sync operations, ~58 warnings cleared per user/month). We scale those by your team size, modeling intensity, and project phase, then apply conservative time-cost values per event:
  • Protection — ~8 min saved per critical block, ~15 min per pin-protect rescue
  • Sync Control — ~10 min saved per prevented collision, ~30% of background-sync time counted as productive
  • Model Health — ~0.5 min saved per warning cleared, ~0.4 min/MB of bloat prevented (team-wide)
  • Activity Tracking — ~20 min recovered per detected crash, billing-accuracy uplift
The high end of the range uses industry-typical time values (≈2× conservative). Ze'Manage license cost is subtracted from gross savings to give net value & payback. Real results depend on your team's discipline mix, model complexity, and how rules are deployed.
